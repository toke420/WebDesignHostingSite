Your first visit

Here's what to expect, so you can walk in relaxed.

  1. Before you arriveYou'll get a short online form by text. Bring your health fund card and any scans.
  2. Assessment (20 min)We talk through your history, goals and test how you move.
  3. Treatment (30 min)Hands-on treatment and your first exercises.
  4. Your planA clear plan with expected timeline and costs, sent to your phone.

Questions

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly. GP Chronic Disease Management referrals are accepted for Medicare rebates.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing that lets us see the area, like shorts for knee problems.

Can I claim on my health fund?

Yes. We claim on the spot with HICAPS so you only pay the gap.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please give 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations may incur a fee.

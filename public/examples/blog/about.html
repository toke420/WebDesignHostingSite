About

A slower kind of magazine.

Field Notes began in 2021 as a letter to friends about the joys of doing things slowly. It now reaches 24,000 readers who'd rather read one good essay than skim fifty headlines.

We publish essays on design, craft, travel and the places we live. Every piece is edited by hand, never optimised for clicks, and free to read thanks to our paying members.

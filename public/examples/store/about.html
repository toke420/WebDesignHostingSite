Our story

Designed in Melbourne. Made to last.

Offbeat started in a Brunswick garage in 2019 with a simple idea: headphones should be repairable, comfortable all day and sound like the studio intended.

Every pair uses replaceable ear cushions, a user-swappable battery and recycled aluminium. When something wears out, we sell the part, not a new pair.

Shipping

Free standard shipping on orders over $150 within Australia. Express delivery is $14.95. We ship to New Zealand for a flat $19.

Returns

Try anything for 60 nights. If it's not right, send it back for a full refund. Return shipping is on us.

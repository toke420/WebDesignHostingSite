Common questions

Can you take over from my current accountant?

Yes. With your permission we request your files directly and handle the hand-over, usually within two weeks.

Do you work with clients outside Melbourne?

About a third of our clients are interstate. Meetings happen by video and documents are shared through our secure portal.

Which software do you support?

Xero, MYOB and QuickBooks Online. We can help you move from spreadsheets or desktop software.

How are your fees charged?

Monthly packages are billed by direct debit. One-off work is quoted upfront, so there are no surprise bills.
